# Study Hub - Your Academic Success Partner

A beautiful, minimal study hub designed to help high school and college students achieve straight A's through organization, motivation, and focus.

## Features

### 🌟 Welcome Experience
- **Time-based Greetings**: Personalized greetings based on your local time (Good Morning, Good Afternoon, Good Evening)
- **Daily Affirmations**: Motivational quotes that change every time you visit or refresh the page
- **Smooth Animations**: Clean, modern interface with smooth transitions

### 📚 Study Hub Dashboard
- **Subject Organization**: Create and manage different subjects (Math, English, Biology, Health, etc.)
- **Task Management**: Add tasks for each subject with checkbox tracking
- **Color-coded Subjects**: Visual organization with customizable colors for each subject
- **Today's Tasks Overview**: See all pending tasks across all subjects in one place

### ⚡ Quick Actions
- **One-Click Google Docs**: Instantly open a blank Google Doc
- **Google Sheets**: Quick access to create spreadsheets
- **Google Slides**: Create presentations with one click

### 🤖 AI Study Assistant
- Get help with:
  - Study techniques and strategies
  - Subject-specific guidance
  - Time management tips
  - Motivation and encouragement
  - Essay writing tips
  - Test preparation strategies

### 💾 Smart Storage
- **Local Storage**: Your subjects, tasks, and preferences are automatically saved
- **No Login Required**: Everything works offline in your browser
- **Privacy First**: All data stays on your device

## How to Use

1. **Open `index.html`** in your web browser
2. **Read your daily affirmation** on the welcome screen
3. **Click "Enter Study Hub"** to access your planner
4. **Add subjects** using the "+ Add Subject" button
5. **Create tasks** for each subject
6. **Use Quick Actions** to open Google Workspace apps
7. **Ask the AI assistant** for study help anytime

## Tips for Success
1. Review your affirmation each morning for motivation
2. Add all your assignments as tasks immediately
3. Check off tasks as you complete them
4. Use the AI assistant when stuck on concepts
5. Keep your subjects organized and up-to-date
